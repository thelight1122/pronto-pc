01 What This Service Is

Pronto PC is an automated AI-assisted Windows repair service operated by 5D Service Solutions Global LLC. Upon payment, you will download and run a lightweight repair agent that scans your system, generates a repair plan, and executes approved actions — all documented in a report delivered to your Desktop.

This is a one-time, flat-rate service for $39 USD. There is no subscription, no recurring charge, and no upsell after payment.

02 What the Agent Will Do

The repair agent will perform only these categories of action:

  • Remove temporary and cached files in standard Windows temp locations
  • Clear browser cache files (Chrome, Edge, Firefox if present)
  • Disable or review unnecessary startup programs
  • Clean orphaned or invalid Windows registry entries
  • Run Windows system integrity checks (SFC) when appropriate
  • Generate a full audit report and save it to your Desktop

Every action will be documented before it executes. You will see a summary of findings before the repair runs.

03 What the Agent Will Never Do

  • Access, move, copy, or delete personal files (documents, photos, music, videos)
  • Collect or transmit passwords, financial data, or any personal information
  • Install third-party applications, toolbars, or browser extensions
  • Modify system drivers, core Windows files, or hardware firmware
  • Make any changes that cannot be reversed or documented
  • Access files outside standard Windows system directories and browser cache paths

04 Your 30-Day Guarantee

If any issue arises that is documented in your audit report within 30 days of your repair, we will perform a second repair at no charge. Email support@prontopc.online with your audit report attached.

We cannot guarantee outcomes for hardware failures, pre-existing software corruption, or issues unrelated to the actions in your audit report. If our diagnosis finds a hardware problem, we will tell you plainly in the report — that diagnosis is included at no extra charge.

05 Refund Policy

If the repair agent fails to run, crashes before completion, or produces no audit report, you are entitled to a full refund. Email support@prontopc.online within 7 days with your payment confirmation.

Refunds are not available if the repair completed and an audit report was delivered. In that case, our re-repair guarantee applies instead.

06 Data & Privacy

The repair agent does not send your personal files, browsing history, or document content to any server. Diagnostic information (disk usage, startup program names, event log error counts) is transmitted only to generate your repair plan and is not stored after your session ends. No personally identifiable information is sold or shared with third parties.

07 AEGIS Governance

This service operates under the AEGIS Alignment Framework by 5D Service Solutions Global LLC. Every action is governed by four commitments: full transparency, your sovereignty over your machine, an append-only unalterable audit trail, and no force or deceptive pricing. These are enforced at the code level.

08 Limitation of Liability

5D Service Solutions Global LLC's total liability for any claim shall not exceed the amount paid ($39). We are not liable for pre-existing conditions, data loss from hardware failure, or any consequential damages. We strongly recommend backing up important files before any repair service.
